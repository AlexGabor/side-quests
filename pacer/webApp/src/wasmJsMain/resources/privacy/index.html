Privacy Policy for Pacer

Effective date: September 13, 2026

Pacer ("the app") is a pace calculator developed by Alex Gabor ("I", "me"). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information collected

Pacer does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or crash reporting, and it does not request network access.

Data stored on your device

Pacer may save your app preferences and settings in the app's private storage on your device. This information never leaves your device and is not included in device backups. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Values you enter

Anything you enter in the app, including values provided through pacer:// links, is processed on your device and is not sent anywhere.

Permissions

Pacer does not request any Android permissions that give it access to personal information, location, contacts, camera, microphone or the internet.

Third parties

No third-party services receive data from the app.

Children's privacy

Pacer collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
